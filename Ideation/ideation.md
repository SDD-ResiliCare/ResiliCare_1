## 2. Unrelated But Highly Innovative New Ideas Proposed (Our IP)

1. **Supply Chain "Clinical Decay Curve" Queue Re-Sorting:** 
   Translating logistics "dynamic perishability" formulas into patient triage. Instead of a static queue, the system actively simulates and re-sorts patient priority every 60 seconds based on wait-time degradation risk.
2. **Keyboard Interaction Stress Analytics:** 
   Our system doesn't wait for a nurse to declare a crisis; it dynamically monitors nurse typing speeds and error rates on the keyboard to passively detect cognitive stress and automatically trigger the aerospace-inspired **"Combat Mode" HUD**.
3. **Continuous Safety Auditing with Demographic Lockouts (The 15% Threshold):** 
   To prevent algorithmic drift and systematic demographic bias, the system continuously audits overrides. If a clinician overrides a specific AI triage rule more than 15% of the time, that specific algorithmic module is **automatically locked down** and flagged for manual engineering review.
4. **Passive Bioacoustic Waiting Room Surveillance:** 
   Using directional ceiling microphones to monitor clinical acoustics (dyspnea, wet coughs, pain pitch frequency) with real-time edge filtering to strip out human language/speech, protecting privacy while catching silent deterioration.
5. **Insurance-Aware Financial Exposure Forecasting During Routing:** 
   Integrating policy parsing (PM-JAY, ESI Scheme, private insurance network eligibility and room-rent caps) directly into the routing and de-escalation engine, ensuring patients are routed to facilities where they maintain cashless eligibility, eliminating proportionate-deduction shortfalls.
